## Vision (draft, from his words above)
- A tutor for a child: it began as spelling for his son (now eight) and now teaches reading and maths too, growing as needed.
- It keeps the child's interest while he learns.
- For his son first, and other children and families too.
- Most important: the tutor works with the child and diagnoses what he gets wrong, rather than giving answers; a parent screen behind a PIN for reports.
- Lines: never end a session in frustration; the child reads little and is not expected to type; children's names never go on chain.
## How feedback is weighed
Feedback that fits this vision is approved and built elegantly; where people differ, a setting; anything crossing a line or changing the vision goes to the Governor.
